The atlas is the table of every work in the survey, with filters on the fields the Chapter 1 charts already use one at a time. A row of the analytic corpus carries the date of the first public version, a link to the primary source, the organizations, the scale (how many agents, and whether that count is simultaneous, cumulative or a capacity), the models, one evidence class and up to three topics. Rows from the chapters' related-work tables are included too, and those rows have fewer metadata fields. Statuses are fixed as of 3 October 2026. Rows for works added on 19 October are marked: they have no evidence class or scale, and their topic is that of the chapter where the card sits.
