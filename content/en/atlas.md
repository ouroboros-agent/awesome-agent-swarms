The atlas gathers all works in the survey into one table. Rows of the analytic corpus — the works behind the charts in Chapter 1 — carry metadata: date, link to the primary source, organizations, scale (how many agents and what exactly was counted), models, evidence class and topics. The atlas also includes the rows of the chapters' related-work tables. Filters above the table select rows by these fields. Dates are those of the first public version; statuses are as of 3 October 2026.
