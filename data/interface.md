# Data interface: canonical work identity and bib keys (for site, catalogue and paper)

Status: **v1.3, 2026-10-09 (reconciled with the integrated paper's `citation-key-map.json`; 18 source-grounded byline/title corrections)** — `data/works.json` (280 records) and `data/bib/works.bib` are generated by `scripts/build_registry.py`. IDs are stable; fields may be filled in, not renamed.
Owner of `data/`: site/catalogue builder. The paper builder (owner of `paper/`) reads these files and does not edit them; requests go through the parent.

## 1. Canonical ID rule

- One **work** = one citable item (paper, preprint, system card, lab blog post, incident report, dataset, repo, essay/interview).
- `id` is a lowercase kebab slug. For every work that already has a card in the October corpus, the ID **is the card ID** (`agensh-1024`, `hf-incident-case`, `kim-scaling-agent-systems`, ...). Old card IDs listed in `build/card_alias.json` are kept as `aliases` and never reused.
- Works that exist only as a row in a "Ещё по теме" table use the October `cards_c` ID when one exists; otherwise a slug of the first significant title words (`zombie-agents`, `moltbook-observatory-archive`, `policysim-based-social-simulation`), frozen in `data/works.json`.
- Other linked resources (news, press releases, company posts) get a slug of host+path (`huggingface-co-blog-security-incident-july-2026`). Hand-named records live in `data/registry_overrides.json` (e.g. `piao-polarization-networks`, `chirper-hashemi-macy`, `micromotor-phase-coherence`).
- **BibTeX key = `id`** (hyphens allowed). The paper cites `\cite{agensh-1024}`. `data/bib/works.bib` will be generated from `data/works.json`; do not hand-edit it.
- Same work, several URLs (arXiv abs/pdf/html/vN, DOI vs publisher page, preprint vs journal version): one ID, the other forms go to `identifiers` / `urls.other`. Example: Piao et al., arXiv:2501.05171 and its Nature Communications version (8 Oct 2026) are **one** work.
- A bundle card (e.g. `hf-incident-case` = OpenAI report + METR/Redwood investigation + HF timeline) keeps one ID for the case; its constituent documents become separate works with `part_of: hf-incident-case` so they can be cited individually.

Constituent documents (`part_of`) use **the paper's component keys** since v1.2 (e.g. `hf-openai-technical-report`, `hf-metr-redwood-investigation`, `hf-huggingface-technical-timeline`, `attractor-states-jakkli-post`, `attractor-states-anthropic-card`, `attractor-states-ko-geiping-paper`, `artificial-hivemind-reevaluation`). The twelve October alias names are aliases of their cards (five synonyms, seven bundle aliases); v1 briefly used seven of them as component IDs; they now resolve to their bundle card, exactly as in the paper's `corpus_aliases`. Bundle cards have `kind: "bundle"`; cite the component documents.

The registry is a superset of the paper bibliography: it also holds bundles, related-table rows, news and other linked resources that the paper does not cite (paper: 261 bibliography entries; registry: see §5).

Post-cut-off update recorded so far: `piao-polarization-networks` (arXiv:2501.05171) = Nature Communications, 8 Oct 2026, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-78228-y, CC BY 4.0 (publisher page read 2026-10-09). The survey text keeps the 3 Oct cut-off; the update is shown as a dated note.

`data/canonical_ids.v0.json` is the superseded early list (all its IDs still exist).

## 3. `data/figures.json` (planned)
