The report's original prose, comparative tables, manually coded evidence synthesis, and the two original conceptual diagrams are by Anton Razzhigaev and Ouroboros and are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), following the owner's accepted release choice.

This grant excludes third-party figures, the inherited raw metadata snapshot, source quotations, and vendored template files. Those materials retain their source-specific rights. The owner authorized preserving all 52 inherited images in the private candidate; this does not assign them an unrestricted reuse license. `figure-rights.json` gives the original and display paths, exact source attribution, inherited rights basis, and hashes for every image. Captions identify the rights of figures actually reproduced in the PDF.

The official ACL files are unmodified. Their original notices and the applicable LPPL reference text are preserved under `vendor/`; `vendor/LICENSE-NOTES.md` records the absence of a repository-wide upstream license at the pinned commit without inventing one.

BibTeX records and the inventory are provenance-bearing derived or copied metadata. The raw inventory remains byte-identical to its source; no new blanket license is asserted for that snapshot. A public release must preserve the component-level distinctions recorded here.
