Abstract

LLM agent collectives combine parallel inference with communication, persistent artifacts, and mechanisms for allocating work. Their outcomes are difficult to compare because agent count, compute budget, organization, and outcome measures change together. This structured narrative review synthesizes research available through 3 October 2026, with a publication-status update to 8 October. Its evidence taxonomy distinguishes model composition, interaction graph, exchange medium, organizational origin, memory, three meanings of scale, baseline controls, and budget–outcome pairs. It identifies conditional benefits, failure mechanisms and open research questions, without proposing a universal scaling law or reporting new experiments.
